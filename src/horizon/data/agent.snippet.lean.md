## Horizon: task continuity

This project is connected to Horizon. It records each task from the user's messages and carries its goal, constraints and the team's rules to later sessions and projects. You don't need to call it.

- **Follow** the team rules and earlier constraints listed at the start of the session, just like the user's own instructions.
- **Optional:** before a hard-to-reverse choice (database, framework, architecture) you may call `evaluate_options`; when the user asks what Horizon remembers or why, use `show_memories` or `explain_decision`.
